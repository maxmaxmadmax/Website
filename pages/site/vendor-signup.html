How do I book a vendor site?

Choose your event, tell us about your business, upload your documents, pick your site on the map and pay. Your site is confirmed once your information and payment have both been received.

How much does a vendor site cost?

A standard 3 m × 3 m site is $50. A larger site with 6 m frontage × 3 m depth is $100. All fees and GST are shown before you pay.

What do I need to upload?

A current Certificate of Currency for public liability insurance (minimum $20 million), plus any licences, permits or food business licence that apply to what you sell. Everything you upload must be genuine, current and valid for the event date.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Once a booking is confirmed and paid, stall fees are non-refundable if you cancel or can’t attend. If the event is postponed for severe weather, your booking transfers to the new date at no extra stall fee. See the for details.

What time can vendors arrive and set up?

Arrive, set up and have your vehicle parked or removed before 4:15 pm. There is no vehicle movement from 4:15 pm until 9:40 pm, and after 9:40 pm only when event staff say the route is clear of the public. Vehicles must never travel through occupied public areas.

Is power or water supplied?

No. Bring your own power and water. Generators must be quiet or silenced models, and all electrical (test-and-tagged where required) and gas equipment must be safe and compliant.

What equipment do I need to bring?

Everything for your stall: marquee, tables, chairs, weights, lighting, leads and weather protection. Ground pegs must be no deeper than 300 mm to protect the underground irrigation, and guy ropes must be safe and clearly visible. You’re responsible for any damage to the irrigation.

What about rubbish and cleaning up?

We provide general bins for the public. You look after your own commercial waste — packaging, food scraps, cooking oil and wastewater — and leave your site clean when you go.

Can I play music or sell alcohol?

No. Please don’t play your own music or use speakers, as it interferes with the live performances. Alcohol is sold only by the authorised event bar. Eatz & Beatz is a family-friendly event.

Can I choose my site?

Yes, you pick your site number during booking. Please stay within your site and don’t swap without approval. For safety or operational reasons we may need to adjust sites, and we’ll offer a suitable alternative where we can.

Do I have to decorate my stall for Halloween?

No, the Halloween theme is optional. But vendors are encouraged to dress up and decorate their stall or food van — for the chance to win their vendor fee back!

Will there be photos and video?

Yes. We may photograph and film the event, and images of your stall, products and branding may be used to promote Eatz & Beatz. Please let your staff know.

Are attendance and sales guaranteed?

No. Eatz & Beatz is a free community event and we promote it hard, but attendance and sales can’t be guaranteed, and fees aren’t refunded for low attendance.

Who can I contact if I have questions?

The Eatz & Beatz team is happy to help with your booking, site requirements or the event — get in touch.
