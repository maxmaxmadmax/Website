Meet the Artists

One big outdoor stage. Live bands and DJs from open to close.

Rossco

Love Island

Straight from the Love Island Australia villa, Rossco is bringing his signature Byron Bay energy to SoundzGood 2.0.

Known for his infectious personality, unmistakable style and love of music, Rossco quickly became one of the standout contestants of Love Island Australia, winning over audiences across the country with his humour, confidence and laid-back vibe.

Now he’s making his way to Bowen for an exclusive appearance at SoundzGood 2.0. Whether you’re a longtime fan of the show or simply looking for an unforgettable day out, this is your chance to meet Rossco in person, grab a photo and experience the energy that made him one of the season’s most talked-about Islanders.

Expect good vibes, plenty of laughs and a special appearance you won’t want to miss.

Jacob Biermann

Triple J Unearthed High Finalist

Gold Coast singer-songwriter Jacob Biermann brings raw emotion, honest storytelling and powerful vocals to the modern indie-pop scene.

He first caught the public’s attention busking on local streets at just 13 years old before becoming a 2021 triple j Unearthed High finalist with his breakout single “Red.”

Since releasing his bedroom-recorded debut “My Kingdom” and acclaimed EP Commodity, Jacob has continued to establish himself as one of Australia’s most exciting emerging artists, combining vulnerable lyrics, infectious hooks and captivating live performances.

Whether performing to an intimate crowd or a festival audience, Jacob delivers an authentic, emotional show that leaves a lasting impression.

Brothas Entertainment — DJ Henasi

Special R&B Set

Representing Brothas Entertainment, DJ Henasi has become one of North Queensland’s most respected open-format DJs, known for delivering unforgettable sets across clubs, festivals and major events.

Blending R&B, hip-hop, dance classics and modern hits, Henasi’s performances are all about creating moments that keep the crowd singing, dancing and wanting more. His ability to seamlessly mix genres has made him a favourite for audiences of all ages.

For SoundzGood 2.0, Henasi is bringing a special R&B set packed with throwback anthems, modern favourites and feel-good energy that’s guaranteed to light up the dance floor.

Nina Sinclair

One of North Queensland’s Leading Female DJs

One of North Queensland’s leading female DJs, Nina Sinclair has built a reputation for delivering high-energy sets that keep dance floors moving from start to finish.

Known for her seamless mixing and ability to read a crowd, Nina blends house, commercial dance, R&B, throwbacks and festival favourites into a set that’s both energetic and unpredictable. Whether she’s performing in packed clubs or at major events across the region, she brings an infectious energy that gets everyone involved.

Making her SoundzGood debut, Nina Sinclair is ready to bring her signature style to Bowen with a festival set designed to keep the party going well into the night.

Pluto Tango

Live Loop Artist

Pluto Tango delivers a live performance unlike anything else on the lineup, creating an entire show in real time using live looping, vocals, guitar and electronic production.

Layer by layer, every beat, melody and vocal is performed live, building immersive soundscapes that blur the line between DJ set and live band. It’s an engaging performance that showcases incredible musicianship while creating an atmosphere that constantly evolves throughout the set.

Whether you’ve seen live looping before or it’s your first time, Pluto Tango brings a unique experience that perfectly complements the diverse lineup at SoundzGood 2.0.

Flaw Clinic

Bowen Icons Return

Originally making his DJ debut at Bowen’s Horseshoe Bay Café in 2022, Flaw Clinic returns home for SoundzGood 2.0 after four years of refining his craft across Townsville’s clubs, festivals and live events.

Since leaving Bowen, he’s shared stages with some of Australia’s biggest electronic acts including Hermitude, Northeast Party House, Bag Raiders, Crooked Colours, Taiki Nulight, Matroda and Little Fritter, building a reputation for high-energy sets that keep crowds moving.

His original Bowen events helped introduce a fresh electronic music scene to Bowen’s nightlife, blending genres and generations into sets that were energetic, unpredictable and always packed the dance floor.

Now, after four more years behind the decks, Flaw Clinic returns to where it all began, ready to bring a bigger and better show to the SoundzGood stage.
