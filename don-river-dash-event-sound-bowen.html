Don River Dash 2026: sound for 3,000 spectators along 300m of river

Off-road trucks, buggies and bikes, 300km of racing in the Don River, and thousands of fans on the banks. Here’s how we made sure every one of them could hear the commentary.

Event
Don River Dash 300
When & where
4–6 Sept 2026, Bowen QLD
Crowd
3,000 across 300m of riverbank

The job

  • River: commentary for the spectators along the course
  • Sponsors’ tent: a 70-inch screen with sound
  • Bowen Showgrounds: PA for the presentations

The challenge

A crowd strung out along 300 metres of river, and power to run out to it. A few big speakers would blast the people up close and lose everyone at the far end.

How we did it

We ran a 100-volt line system, the setup used at racecourses and stadiums, with 40 horn speakers spaced along the bank. Every spectator got clear commentary at a comfortable level, wherever they stood. Four 12-inch speakers handled the presentations at the showgrounds.

The result

“Best one yet” was the word after the event, and we’ll be back for the Dash in 2027.
