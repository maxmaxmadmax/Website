When and where is the Eatz & Beatz Halloween Market?

Saturday 31 October 2026, from 4:00 pm to 10:00 pm, at the Bowen Sporting Complex.

How do I book a vendor site?

Head to our events page, choose the Eatz & Beatz Halloween Market, fill in the vendor form and follow the prompts to pay for your site.

How much does a vendor site cost?

A standard 3 m × 3 m site is $50. A larger site with 6 m frontage × 3 m depth is $100.

When is my booking confirmed?

Once your vendor information and your payment have both been received.

What information do I need to provide?

Your business and contact details, the products or food you’ll be selling, the site size you need and any power requirements.

Is power supplied?

No — power is not supplied at your site. Every vendor brings their own, so plan for a generator or battery power for anything you need to run.

What time can vendors arrive and set up?

Arrival, set-up and vehicle-access instructions are emailed to confirmed vendors closer to the event.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

Yes. Bring everything you need for your setup — marquee, tables, chairs, lighting, extension leads and weather protection where it applies.

Do I have to decorate my stall for Halloween?

No, the Halloween theme is optional. But vendors are encouraged to dress up and decorate their stall or food van — for the chance to win their vendor fee back!

Who can I contact if I have questions?

The Eatz & Beatz team is happy to help with your booking, site requirements or the event — get in touch.
