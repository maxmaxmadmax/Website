Is there power or water at my site?

No. There is no power or water connection anywhere on the vendor sites for this event, so bring a generator and whatever water you need for the day. We ask which generator you're bringing later on, because we place louder ones away from the main stage to keep the noise down for everyone.

Can I sell alcohol or run a bar?

No. SoundzGood runs the bar at this event, so we're not taking bar or alcohol vendors.

How big a space can I take?

A food vendor site is a single 6 m × 3 m space. Market stalls are sold by the 3 m × 3 m bay and you can join up to 8 of them together, so you choose your own size and shape on the map — 1 bay for a small stall, or a long run for a big one. You pay per bay.

Why are some spaces greyed out on the map?

We release sites a few at a time, closest in first, so the market fills up without gaps between stalls. As the open ones go, the next lot opens automatically. If your stall needs more room than the open bays give you, it can still run on into the greyed-out ones — just start from an open bay.

What documents do I need?

Public liability insurance for every vendor, and food vendors also need their food licence. A PDF or a clear photo is fine, up to 10 MB each. You upload them as part of this form.

Why is there a limit on my category?

We cap how many vendors sell the same thing so everyone trades well and patrons get a proper mix. Once a category fills it closes off and you'll need to pick another.

How long do I have to pay?

Once you choose your site it's held for you for 10 minutes while you finish up and pay. If you don't get through checkout in that time the site goes back on the map for someone else — you can simply pick it again if it's still free.

When do I find out my bump-in time?

Closer to the event. We'll email your arrival time, which gate to use and where your site is once the full site plan is locked in.
