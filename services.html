Event Production

More Than Just Hire.

SoundzGood provides complete event production across Bowen, the Whitsundays and North Queensland.

From small community events and weddings through to festivals, sporting events and outdoor productions, we can provide the sound, lighting, staging, LED screens and technical crew required to bring an event together.

Core Capabilities

  • Audio

    Professional PA systems, microphones and live-event audio.

  • Lighting

    Stage lighting, event lighting and architectural lighting.

  • Staging

    Mobile staging and our trailer stage.

  • LED Screens

    Outdoor LED screens, scoreboards and video playback.

  • Backline & DJ

    DJ systems, drums, amplifiers and artist equipment.

  • Technical Crew

    Audio engineers, lighting operators and event technicians.

Event Production Across North Queensland

SoundzGood is based in Bowen and provides event production, PA systems, lighting, LED screens, staging and technical support across the Whitsundays and North Queensland.

We regularly work across Bowen, Airlie Beach, Proserpine, Mackay, Townsville, Collinsville and the surrounding regional areas.
