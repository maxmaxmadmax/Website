FAQs

Good to know.

Everything you need before you roll in.

  • Do I need a ticket?

    Nope! Eatz & Beatz is free entry - just turn up.

  • Is it family friendly?

    Absolutely. Bring the kids and enjoy great food, markets, live music and Halloween fun for all ages.

  • Should I bring a chair?

    Yes! Bring a camp chair or picnic rug and settle in for the night.

  • Can I bring food or drinks?

    No outside food or drinks please. Come hungry and support our local food vendors and bar.

  • Can I bring alcohol?

    No BYO alcohol. There will be a licensed bar operating on the night.

  • Do we have to dress up?

    No - but we highly recommend it! There’ll be best dressed prizes for kids and adults.

  • What’s happening for the kids?

    Trick or treating around the market stalls, plus plenty of food, markets and entertainment.

  • Where do I park?

    Plenty of parking available at the Bowen Sports Complex.
