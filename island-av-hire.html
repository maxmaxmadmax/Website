How it gets there

We have our own boat.

Deliveries go on our vessel rather than on somebody else’s timetable, which is the difference between planning a day around a ferry and planning it around your ceremony. Heavier loads — staging, LED screens — go by barge.

Whitehaven Beach

No power? No problem.

Whitehaven has no mains power and no generators humming behind the vows. We run a battery setup: PA and microphones for the ceremony and the speeches, carried in, run clean, carried out.

What We’ll Ask You

An island event is a mainland event plus a boat. Four things decide it.

  1. The island and the spot

    Where on it, and how far the gear travels once it lands.

  2. Power, or none

    What the venue has — and if the answer is nothing, that is fine.

  3. Access and timing

    When we can land and when everything has to be off again.

  4. The wet plan

    What happens to an outdoor setup if the wind comes up. Worth settling early.
